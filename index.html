2-Minute Snack Hacks

Elevate your Poha Savoury pouch into a quick, delicious dish.

Dahi Poha Chaat

Chilled whisked curd, mint chutney, and a generous layer of crisp Poha Savoury.

Ingredients

  • 1 Pouch KB Poha Savoury
  • 1/2 cup Whisked Curd (Dahi)
  • 1 tbsp Mint & Tamarind Chutney
  • Fresh Coriander

Preparation

  1. Pour chilled curd into a wide bowl.
  2. Drizzle mint and tamarind chutneys over the curd.
  3. Top with KB Poha Savoury right before eating for peak crunch.

South Indian Bhel Mix

Chopped onions, juicy tomatoes, raw mango, and fresh lemon juice combined with Poha Savoury.

Ingredients

  • 1 Pouch KB Poha Savoury
  • 1/4 cup Chopped Onions & Tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp Grated Raw Mango
  • Squeeze of Lemon & Chaat Masala

Preparation

  1. Combine fresh veggies and raw mango in a mixing bowl.
  2. Toss gently with KB Poha Savoury.
  3. Squeeze lemon juice, sprinkle chaat masala, and serve immediately.

Avocado Toast Topper

Smashed avocado on toasted sourdough, finished with spicy, crunchy Poha Savoury.

Ingredients

  • 1 Pouch KB Poha Savoury
  • 1 Slice Toasted Sourdough or Whole Wheat
  • 1/2 Ripe Avocado (mashed)
  • Chili Flakes & Sea Salt

Preparation

  1. Spread ripe mashed avocado onto hot crispy toast.
  2. Season with chili flakes and a pinch of salt.
  3. Cover heavily with KB Poha Savoury for texture contrast.

Spiced Peanut Butter Toast

Creamy peanut butter on crisp toast crowned with a savory, crunchy layer of Poha Savoury.

Ingredients

  • 1 Pouch KB Poha Savoury
  • 1 Slice Bread (Toasted)
  • 2 tbsp Natural Peanut Butter
  • A pinch of Black Pepper or Honey

Preparation

  1. Spread an even layer of creamy peanut butter over warm toast.
  2. Optionally drizzle a tiny drop of honey or pinch of pepper.
  3. Press a generous handful of KB Poha Savoury on top for a sweet-savory crunch.

Caesar Crunch Salad

Crisp romaine lettuce, creamy Caesar dressing, parmesan shavings, and a crown of savory, spiced KB Poha Savoury.

Ingredients

  • 1 Pouch KB Poha Savoury
  • 2 cups Chopped Romaine Lettuce
  • 2 tbsp Caesar Dressing
  • 1 tbsp Grated Parmesan Cheese
  • Freshly cracked black pepper

Preparation

  1. Toss chopped romaine lettuce with Caesar dressing in a mixing bowl until evenly coated.
  2. Transfer to a serving plate and garnish with freshly grated parmesan cheese.
  3. Heap a generous handful of KB Poha Savoury right on top just before serving for the ultimate crunch.
