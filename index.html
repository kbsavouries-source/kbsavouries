Storage Instructions

To preserve the authentic taste and ensure these treats last until their expiration date, please follow these guidelines:

  • 📦 Poha Crunch & Sunnundalu (Ulundu Urundai): Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place. Keep away from moisture.
  • 🌶️ Idly Podi & Gongura Chutney: Store in a cool place. Note: We highly recommend refrigerating the Gongura Chutney after opening and always using a clean, dry spoon to prevent spoilage.
