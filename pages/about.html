About KB Savouries

Fresh from Kitchen, Not Factory.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are KB Savouries snacks prepared in fresh kitchen batches?

Yes — every single batch is prepared fresh in our kitchen following traditional methods. We never rely on factory mass production or automated stock piling, ensuring authentic home taste and crispness.

Are your snacks 100% vegan and free from preservatives?

Absolutely. All our savouries are 100% plant-based/vegan, crafted without any artificial preservatives, chemical additives, or hydrogenated oils.

Where do you ship, and how long does delivery take?

We ship all across Tamil Nadu with reliable courier partners. Standard orders are packed fresh and dispatched within 3 to 5 days.

Who makes KB Savouries?

KB Savouries is crafted with passion in small dedicated kitchen batches, preserving age-old culinary secrets and authentic flavours for your tea-time enjoyment.
