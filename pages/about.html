Frequently Asked Questions

Are all products 100% vegan?

Yes! Every single product at KB Savouries is 100% vegan, containing zero dairy, ghee, or animal-based fats.

How should I store my savouries after opening?

Store your Poha Crunch in a cool, dry place inside an airtight container. For items like Gongura Chutney, refrigeration after opening is strongly recommended.

Do you accept bulk orders for gifting or events?

Absolutely! We specialize in custom bulk quantities for family gatherings, festive gifting, and corporate events. Contact us directly via WhatsApp to discuss your requirement.
