How to Install
KB Savouries App

Get fast access, instant ordering, and smooth performance right from your home screen without downloading from the App Store or Play Store.

STEP 1

Tap the Browser Menu

Open kb-savouries.web.app in Chrome or your mobile browser. Tap the three vertical dots (⠇) in the top-right corner of your browser screen.

STEP 2

Select "Install and create shortcut"

In the browser options menu, scroll down and select Install and create shortcut (or "Add to Home screen").

STEP 3

Confirm "Install"

A popup sheet will appear at the bottom of your screen. Tap Install to add the KB Savouries app icon directly to your home screen!

🍎

Using an iPhone / Safari?

Tap the Share button at the bottom of your Safari browser, scroll down the options list, and tap "Add to Home Screen".
